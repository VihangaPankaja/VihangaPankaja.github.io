Organizational Behavior and Management

A guided route through people, teams, and organizations at work.

Big pictureStudy routeHow to use the guide

Big picture

Organizational behavior examines how people think, feel, and act at work, and how teams and organizations shape those experiences. This guide follows the course topics in prerequisite order and connects concepts to workplace situations.
